SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT & MAINTENANCE AGREEMENT

This Software Development & Maintenance Agreement ("Agreement") is made between
Pinnacle Digital Labs LLC ("Developer") and the undersigned client ("Client") as
of the Effective Date. By signing, the Client agrees to the following terms.

1. SERVICES AND DEPOSIT
Developer shall design and build the software described in the Statement of Work.
Client shall pay a non-refundable deposit equal to fifty percent (50%) of the total
project fee before any work begins. The deposit is non-refundable under all
circumstances, including if the Client terminates the project for any reason.

2. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All intellectual property, source code, designs, and deliverables created under this
Agreement shall remain the sole and exclusive property of Developer until the Client
has paid all amounts owed in full. Notwithstanding payment, Developer retains a
perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide license to reuse, resell, and sublicense any code,
components, or know-how developed for the Client, including the Client's
contributed materials and business logic.

3. MAINTENANCE RETAINER AND AUTO-RENEWAL
Upon delivery, the Client is automatically enrolled in a monthly maintenance retainer
of $4,500 per month. This retainer renews automatically for successive twelve (12)
month terms unless the Client provides written notice of cancellation at least ninety
(90) days before the end of the then-current term. Early cancellation requires payment
of fifty percent (50%) of all remaining retainer fees as liquidated damages.

4. EXCLUSIVITY
During the term of this Agreement and for two (2) years thereafter, the Client shall
not engage, hire, or contract any other software developer, agency, or freelancer for
any work similar to the Services without Developer's prior written consent.

5. INDEMNIFICATION
The Client shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Developer, its officers, and
agents from any and all claims, damages, losses, and expenses of any kind arising out
of or relating to the software or the Client's business, without limitation and
regardless of cause, including Developer's own negligence.

6. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
The Services and all deliverables are provided "AS IS" without warranty of any kind.
Developer's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed one hundred dollars
($100), and Developer shall not be liable for any defects, downtime, data loss, or
damages of any kind.

7. PERSONAL GUARANTEE
The individual signing on behalf of the Client personally and unconditionally
guarantees all of the Client's obligations under this Agreement, including all fees,
retainers, and liquidated damages, and waives any right to require Developer to proceed
against the Client first.

8. CONFIDENTIALITY
The Client shall keep all terms of this Agreement and all information about Developer
strictly confidential in perpetuity. This obligation binds the Client only and does
not apply to Developer.

9. LATE PAYMENT
Any amount not paid when due shall accrue interest at five percent (5%) per month, and
Developer may suspend all services and revoke all licenses immediately upon any late
payment.

10. GOVERNING LAW
