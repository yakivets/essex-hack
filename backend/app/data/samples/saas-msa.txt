MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of the Effective Date
between Acme Corp ("Customer") and Globex SaaS, Inc. ("Provider") for an initial term of
twenty-four (24) months.

1. TERM & RENEWAL
This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive twenty-four (24) month periods
unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days prior
to the end of the then-current term.

2. FEES
Customer shall pay the fees set forth in the applicable Order Form. Fees are due quarterly
in advance. Provider may modify pricing upon thirty (30) days' written notice to Customer.
Overdue invoices accrue interest at 1.5% per month, and Provider may suspend the Services
if any invoice is more than fifteen (15) days past due.

3. INDEMNIFICATION
Customer shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Provider from any and all third-party
claims arising out of Customer's use of the Services, without limitation.

4. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
Provider's total aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid by
Customer in the three (3) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

5. DATA & PRIVACY
Provider shall process Customer Data in accordance with its Privacy Policy, as updated from
time to time at Provider's discretion.

6. SERVICE LEVELS
Provider targets 99.9% monthly uptime, excluding scheduled maintenance. No service credits
or other remedies are provided for failure to meet this target.

7. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State
of Delaware, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts located
therein.

8. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach upon thirty (30) days' written
notice and an opportunity to cure. Termination for convenience is not permitted.
