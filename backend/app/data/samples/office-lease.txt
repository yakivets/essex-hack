COMMERCIAL OFFICE LEASE

This Lease is made between Sterling Properties LLC ("Landlord") and the Tenant for the
premises described in Schedule A, for a term of thirty-six (36) months.

1. RENT
Tenant shall pay base rent monthly in advance. Base rent increases by four percent (4%)
on each anniversary of the commencement date.

2. COMMON AREA MAINTENANCE
Tenant shall pay its proportionate share of Common Area Maintenance ("CAM") charges,
including taxes, insurance, and operating expenses, with no annual cap on increases.

3. PERSONAL GUARANTEE
The individual signing on behalf of Tenant personally and unconditionally guarantees all
obligations under this Lease, including rent for the full term.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Tenant shall provide a security deposit equal to three (3) months' base rent, which Landlord
may apply to any default and is not required to hold in a separate account.

5. ASSIGNMENT & SUBLETTING
Tenant may not assign or sublet the premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which
may be withheld in Landlord's sole discretion.

6. REPAIRS
Tenant is responsible for all repairs and maintenance of the premises, including HVAC and
structural elements within the leased space.

7. EARLY TERMINATION
There is no right of early termination. If Tenant vacates early, Tenant remains liable for
rent for the remainder of the term.

8. GOVERNING LAW
This Lease is governed by the laws of the State of New York.
