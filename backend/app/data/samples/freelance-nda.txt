MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is made between Northwind Ltd and the
Recipient as of the Effective Date.

1. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by either party,
whether marked confidential or not, that a reasonable person would understand to be
confidential.

2. OBLIGATIONS
Each party agrees to use the other's Confidential Information solely to evaluate a potential
business relationship and to protect it with at least the same care it uses for its own
confidential information, but no less than reasonable care.

3. TERM
The confidentiality obligations in this Agreement survive for a period of five (5) years
from the date of disclosure.

4. NON-SOLICITATION
For twelve (12) months, neither party shall solicit for employment any employee of the other
party with whom it had contact during the evaluation.

5. RETURN OF MATERIALS
Upon written request, each party shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential
Information of the other party.

6. NO LICENSE
Nothing in this Agreement grants any license or ownership rights in either party's
intellectual property.

7. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
