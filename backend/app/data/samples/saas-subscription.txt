SAAS SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This SaaS Subscription Agreement ("Agreement") is between Northwind Analytics Inc.
("Provider") and the customer identified in the Order Form ("Customer"). It governs
Customer's subscription to Provider's cloud analytics platform (the "Service").

1. SUBSCRIPTION AND TERM
Provider grants Customer a non-exclusive subscription to access the Service for the
initial term stated in the Order Form (the "Initial Term"), beginning on the start
date set out there.

2. FEES
Customer shall pay the subscription fees set out in the Order Form annually in advance.
Provider shall invoice Customer at the start of each term, and undisputed invoices are
due within thirty (30) days.

3. RENEWAL
This Agreement renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month terms unless
either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days before the
end of the then-current term. Provider may increase the subscription fees by up to
seven percent (7%) for each renewal term.

4. SERVICE LEVELS
Provider will use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Service available 99.5%
of the time each month, excluding scheduled maintenance. If availability falls below
this target, Customer's sole remedy is a service credit of up to ten percent (10%) of
the monthly fee, applied on request.

5. CUSTOMER DATA
Customer retains ownership of the data it uploads to the Service ("Customer Data").
Provider may use aggregated and de-identified Customer Data to operate, analyse, and
improve its products and services, and to develop new features. Provider processes
Customer Data in accordance with its standard Data Processing Addendum.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY
Each party shall protect the other's confidential information with reasonable care and
use it only to perform under this Agreement. These obligations are mutual and survive
for three (3) years after termination.

7. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
Provider's total aggregate liability arising out of this Agreement shall not exceed the
fees paid by Customer in the three (3) months preceding the event giving rise to
the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages.

8. ASSIGNMENT
Provider may assign or transfer this Agreement, in whole or in part, to any affiliate
or successor in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets. Customer may
not assign this Agreement without Provider's prior written consent.

9. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement for the other party's material breach that
remains uncured thirty (30) days after written notice. On termination, Customer's
access to the Service ends and Provider will make Customer Data available for export
for thirty (30) days, after which it may be deleted.

10. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, and the parties submit
to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts located in New York County.
