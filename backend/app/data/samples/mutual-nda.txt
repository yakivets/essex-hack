MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between the two
undersigned parties (each a "Party" and together the "Parties") to enable discussions
about a possible business relationship. The Parties agree as follows.

1. PURPOSE
Each Party may disclose certain confidential information to the other solely to
evaluate a potential business relationship (the "Purpose").

2. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
"Confidential Information" means non-public information that is marked confidential or
that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, disclosed by one Party
(the "Discloser") to the other (the "Recipient"). It does not include information that
is or becomes public through no fault of the Recipient, was lawfully known before
disclosure, is independently developed without use of the Confidential Information, or
is lawfully received from a third party.

3. OBLIGATIONS
Each Party agrees to use the other Party's Confidential Information only for the
Purpose, to protect it with the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential
information (and no less than reasonable care), and to disclose it only to its
employees and advisors who need to know it and are bound by similar confidentiality
obligations. These obligations are identical and reciprocal for both Parties.

4. COMPELLED DISCLOSURE
A Recipient may disclose Confidential Information if required by law or court order,
provided it gives the Discloser prompt written notice (where legally permitted) so the
Discloser may seek protective treatment.

5. NO LICENCE OR TRANSFER
All Confidential Information remains the property of the Discloser. Nothing in this
Agreement grants either Party any licence or ownership rights in the other Party's
intellectual property. No Party is obligated to proceed with any transaction.

6. TERM AND SURVIVAL
This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for two (2) years. Each
Party's confidentiality obligations survive for two (2) years after disclosure of the
relevant Confidential Information.

7. RETURN OR DESTRUCTION
Upon written request or termination, each Recipient shall promptly return or destroy
the other Party's Confidential Information, except for one archival copy retained for
legal compliance.

8. NO WARRANTY; LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
All Confidential Information is provided "as is." Each Party's liability under this
Agreement is limited to direct damages, and this limitation applies equally to both
Parties.

9. GENERAL
Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other's prior written consent,
which shall not be unreasonably withheld. Either Party may terminate this Agreement
on thirty (30) days' written notice. This Agreement is governed by the laws of England
and Wales, and the Parties submit to the non-exclusive jurisdiction of its courts.
